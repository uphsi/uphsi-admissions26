AY 2027–2028

Grade 7 Admissions

Applications: September 7–October 9, 2026 · Entrance Examination: October 17, 2026

Screening

Requirement for Screening

  • Applicants must have a weighted average grade of 80 or better for Grade 4 and Grade 5.
  • Initial qualifiers will be required to take the UPHSI JHS Entrance Examination.
Documents

Required Documents

  • Grades 4 and 5 Report Cards certified as true copy by the School Principal
  • Two (2) pieces 2" × 2" photo taken within the last 6 months
  • Photocopy of Birth Certificate from PSA
  • Photograph of the applicant in front of his/her house with full front exterior visible
  • Information sheet duly accomplished and jointly signed by the student applicant and his/her parent
  • Documents on family income
Important: Use and submit up-to-date admission forms. Older versions of the forms will not be accepted. Secure, attach, and review all requirements before submitting for evaluation to avoid delays.
